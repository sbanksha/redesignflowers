Hi, I'm Ellie Mae.

My love for flowers began young, shaped by art classes and my family's gardens, something I have always gathered, grown and created with.

In 2020 I designed my first wedding and was instantly hooked. I went full-time in 2021, working in a wholesale shop and alongside talented florists. After welcoming my son, Atticus, in 2023, I launched Sparrow & Lily.

I am a Tennessee native, living on two peaceful acres with my husband and our two children.
