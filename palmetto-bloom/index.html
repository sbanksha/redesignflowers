Services

A consultation, mood boards and a custom proposal, with installations and rentals like arches and pedestals, vessels and candles. Delivery, setup and takedown are included.

About

Gardens, landscape design and a studio with history.

Palmetto Bloom was founded in Charleston in 2015 by Jonie Larosee. Owner and creative director Colleen Purdy Tangeman, a gardener and landscape-design enthusiast with 20+ years as an English teacher and program director, took the helm and brings the same care to helping couples plan their flowers with confidence.

Jonie remains part of the Palmetto Bloom family.
