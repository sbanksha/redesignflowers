§ 04   Process

4chapters,
one crew.

  1. I

    12–9 months out

    Consult

    A ninety-minute call, then we walk the room at the hour you’ll actually be in it. Four p.m. light and ten p.m. light are two different weddings.

  2. II

    6 months out

    Design

    One proposal, not three. Then a full mock-up table at the studio: real linens, real candles, the wine you picked. You tell us what’s wrong. Something always is.

  3. III

    The day, from 6 a.m.

    Install

    Crew of four to eleven. Six to nine hours. Ines stays until the first course goes out, then fixes the one centrepiece someone leaned on.

  4. IV

    Midnight

    Strike

    We take every stem down. Spent material goes to compost in Red Hook; anything still good is re-bunched and at two Park Slope hospices by ten the next morning.

“Ines turned a parking garage into a chapel, then had it swept clean by one a.m. I’ve stopped sending couples anywhere else.”

Maren Okafor — planner, Okafor & Lane Events · eleven weddings with Ossia since 2021
