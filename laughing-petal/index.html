Hi, I'm Anna.

I was born and raised in Sylva, North Carolina, and moved to Nashville in 2003. My mother set up florals for weddings and I spent hours in her studio playing with flowers.

When the pandemic hit, I missed having a creative outlet. I started gathering grocery-store flowers and delivering arrangements, and The Laughing Petal grew from there.
