The bloom bar

Three steps.
Zero skills needed.

  1. 1

    Pick your stems

    Rows of white buckets, full of loose stems. Guests walk up and pull whatever they like.

  2. 2

    Build it

    All pink, all yellow, one of everything. There is no wrong bouquet.

  3. 3

    We wrap it

    Hand it to our attendant. It comes back wrapped in kraft paper, ready to carry home.
