About

Rose + Lane.

Rose Lane Floral Design was established in 2014 by lead designer Corinne Schaffer, who builds every arrangement on the principles of proportion, scale, harmony, rhythm, balance, unity and emphasis.

Rose and Lane are the middle names of her two daughters. In 2015 Corinne began designing larger events: weddings, showers, galas and bat mitzvahs.
