About

Florals shaped by farmland, produce and a love of nature.

Maria Jenkins grew up in Trent Woods, near New Bern, NC, close to her grandparents’ farmland and tree business, and worked in the produce world supporting growers before following her path into flowers. Moonlight Iris began in January 2015, spent eight years in Charleston, and is now headquartered in Wilmington, NC.

“From the mountains to the sea, there is no greater source than Mother Nature.” — Maria Jenkins
