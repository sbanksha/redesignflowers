About

Meet Mollie.

Mollie Johnson started Hummingbirds to feed a genuine love for giving. She came to flowers by way of a biology degree, and it shows: every bouquet is worked out in color, shape and scent.

She’s Pace and Pensacola through and through. As her family has grown, so has the shop: weddings, a Flower School, kids’ crafts on Saturday mornings, and homecoming corsages for Tate, Gulf Breeze and Central.

Her favorite thing in the world? Putting smiles on people’s faces.
