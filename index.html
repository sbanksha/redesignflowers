We only touch what your customers see.

What changes

  • How your shop looks, on every screen
  • Your best photos actually on the homepage
  • Pages that load fast on a phone
  • Inquiries that arrive with date, venue and budget
  • Your name looks like you mean it on Google

What stays exactly the same

  • Your ordering system: Shopify, Square, BloomNation, WhatsApp
  • Your domain
  • Your email (we never touch it)
  • Your prices, products and payouts
  • Your Instagram. It’s already great.

How it works

Seven days, start to live.

  1. Day 0

    We build your demo first

    Before you pay anything, we build a working version of your new site from your public photos and info. If we messaged you, it already exists.

  2. Days 1–6

    You tell us what’s off

    One round of changes: the photos you love, the wording you’d never use, the page you need. Ten minutes of your time, maybe fifteen.

  3. Day 7

    We flip the switch

    We point your domain at the new site. Orders keep flowing through the system you already use. Nobody has to learn anything.

Questions florists ask us

Do I have to switch my ordering system?

No. That’s the point. Your Order buttons link to the checkout you already use (Shopify, Square, BloomNation, Teleflora, or a WhatsApp chat), so payments, payouts and your daily routine don’t change.

What does it cost?

One flat price, agreed before we start: half to begin, half the day it goes live. No monthly fee, unless you later want us to handle updates.

Will my email stop working?

No. We only change the record that points your domain at the website. Your email settings stay exactly where they are.

Do you need my passwords?

Never. The demo is built from public info. After you say yes, you add us as a collaborator or we do a ten-minute screen share. You stay the owner of everything.

Who owns the site?

You do: the files, the design, the domain. If you ever want to leave, you take it all with you.

Can you write it in Spanish?

Sí. Sites can be Spanish-first, English-first, or both with a toggle.
