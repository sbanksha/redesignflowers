A business of revelers

Carly Blair started House of Margot Blair in her downtown Austin apartment in 2009, becoming the go-to florist and event planner for couples who wanted something at once edgy and romantic. Demand grew past weddings, and so did the company: today it covers every part of luxury entertaining, no matter the occasion.
